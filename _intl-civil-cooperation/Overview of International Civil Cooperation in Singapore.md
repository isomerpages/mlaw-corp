<p>The information in this section relates to international cooperation for
court or tribunal proceedings involving non-criminal matters only. For
information on international criminal cooperation, please see <a href="https://www.agc.gov.sg/our-roles/public-prosecutor/mutual-legal-assistance/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
<p>This site focuses on two main areas of international civil cooperation:
(a) obtaining evidence across borders, and (b) serving legal documents
across borders.</p>
<p>More information is available at the following pages:</p>
<p><strong>(a) Obtaining evidence across borders:</strong>
</p>
<p>i. <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/obtaining-evidence-in-singapore-for-foreign-proceedings/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Obtaining Evidence in Singapore for Foreign Proceedings</a>
</p>
<p>ii. <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/obtaining-voluntary-evidence-remotely-from-a-person-in-singapore-for-foreign-proceedings/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Obtaining Voluntary Evidence Remotely from a Person in Singapore for Foreign Proceedings</a>
</p>
<p>iii. <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/obtaining-evidence-overseas-for-singapore-court-proceedings/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Obtaining Evidence Overseas for Singapore Court Proceedings</a>
</p>
<p><strong>(b) Serving legal documents across borders:</strong>
</p>
<p>i. <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/service-of-foreign-process-in-singapore/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Service of Foreign Process in Singapore</a>
</p>
<p>ii. <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/service-out-of-singapore-of-singapore-court-documents/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Service out of Singapore of Singapore Court Documents</a>
</p>
<p>All Singapore legislation referred to on this site may be found at <a href="https://sso.agc.gov.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Singapore Statutes Online</a>.</p>
<p>Please note that the Singapore Government is not in a position to provide
legal advice, including in relation to any underlying proceedings.</p>
