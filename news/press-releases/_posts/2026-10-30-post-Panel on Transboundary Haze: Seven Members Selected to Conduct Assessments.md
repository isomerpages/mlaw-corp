30 October 2026 Posted in [Press releases](/news/press-releases)
     Kroll    

1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Seven members will be appointed to the Panel on Transboundary Haze. They are investigations firms Control Risks Group (S) Pte Ltd., FTI Consulting (Singapore) Pte. Ltd., Kroll Singapore Pte. Ltd. and law firms[\[1\]](#_ftn1)/legal advisors Dentons Rodyk &amp; Davidson LLP, Toby Landau KC of Duxton Hill Chambers, TSMP Law Corporation and WongPartnership LLP. They bring expertise in forensic investigations, financial analysis, corporate intelligence, risk advisory, and legal services.

2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; As set out in the Government's earlier announcement, the Government has appointed Davinder Singh Chambers (DSC LLC) as Advisor. DSC LLC will also coordinate the work done by the Panel firms to ensure that their evaluation reports effectively address the issues and contain the information and evidence needed by the National Environment Agency (NEA) for its assessment.&nbsp;

3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Companies identified by the Government have been asked to engage a team selected by NEA, comprising an investigations firm and a law firm/legal advisor from the Panel. NEA will ask the assessors to evaluate the companies’ operations, corporate relationships and supply chains. Companies are expected to cooperate fully.

4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The assessment will be conducted at the companies’ own expenses. The assessors will submit an evaluation report to NEA, which will take the findings into account in determining whether further action is warranted.

5.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The Government intends to table amendments to the Transboundary Haze Pollution Act 2014 at the next Parliamentary sitting to give legal force to this framework.
