**Official Visit to China by Senior Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Law and Ministry of Social and Family Development Eric Chua**

1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Senior Parliamentary Secretary (“SPS”) of the Ministry of Law and the Ministry of Social and Family Development Eric Chua made an official visit to Hainan, China from 15 to 16 October 2026.

2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; During his visit, SPS Chua delivered a speech at the Opening Ceremony of the Hainan Free Trade Port Legal Week 2026, which also saw strong participation from the Singapore’s legal community. In his speech, he highlighted the complementary strengths of Singapore and Hainan as open trading economies as well as the growing legal cooperation between the two cities. He also reiterated the importance of a stable and transparent legal environment, supported by reliable and efficient dispute resolution mechanisms. A transcript of SPS Chua's speech is available at [https://go.gov.sg/spshaikou](https://go.gov.sg/spshaikou)

3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; SPS Chua also paid a courtesy call on Hainan Provincial Party Committee’s Political and Legal Affairs Commission Deputy Secretary and Chief Procurator Zhang Yi, and met with key leaders and stakeholders from Hainan and other cities.

4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; SPS Chua was accompanied by officials from the Ministry of Law.
