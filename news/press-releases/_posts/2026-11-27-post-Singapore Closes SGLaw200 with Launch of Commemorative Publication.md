<p><strong>Singapore Closes SGLaw200 with Launch of Commemorative Publication, "One People, One Nation, One Rule of Law"</strong>
</p>
<p>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; On 27 November 2026, Singapore closed its year-long
commemoration of SGLaw200, the bicentennial of Singapore’s modern legal
and judicial system since the enactment of the Second Charter of Justice
in 1826, with the launch of "One People, One Nation, One Rule of Law" —
a commemorative publication tracing two centuries of the Rule of Law and
the questions it must answer in the century ahead. The publication was
launched by Senior Minister, Coordinating Minister for National Security
and Minister for Home Affairs Mr K Shanmugam, Chief Justice Sundaresh Menon,
and Minister for Law and Second Minister for Home Affairs Mr Edwin Tong
SC at an appreciation reception, where participants from across the legal
and wider community gathered to mark the occasion and recognise all those
who had contributed to bringing SGLaw200 to life over the past year.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong><u>A Year of Commemoration Across the Nation</u></strong>
</p>
<p>2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; SGLaw200 was officially announced at the 37th
Singapore Law Review Annual by Senior Minister of State Mr Murali Pillai
SC in Jan 2026. Over the year, the legal fraternity, law schools, and community
partners came together to bring the story of the Rule of Law across the
island through public outreach, youth engagement, and community initiatives.
Key highlights included:</p>
<p></p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong><em>The Next Charter: Shaping Singapore's Legal Future Together</em></strong><em> on 6 March 2026</em>,
a first-of-its-kind gathering of over 1,000 members of Singapore's legal
community to discuss the next chapter of Singapore's legal journey.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The <strong><em>SGLaw200 Youth Forum</em></strong><em> on 13 May 2026</em>,
which convened young Singaporeans to discuss issues shaping the Rule of
Law, with a welcome address by Prime Minister Mr Lawrence Wong and a dialogue
with Minister Mr Edwin Tong SC and members of the judiciary.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>&nbsp;The <strong><em>SGLaw200 Justice Float</em></strong> at the National
Day Parade in August 2026, honouring individuals who uphold justice in
everyday community life and underscoring that the Rule of Law belongs to
every Singaporean.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><em>"Laws That Shaped Our Nation"</em></strong>, a two-part documentary
series produced by Channel News Asia in partnership with the Ministry of
Law, tracing how Singapore's law has evolved alongside the nation. The
series was broadcast in November 2026 and is available on-demand at:
<a href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/watch/laws-shaped-our-nation" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Laws That Shaped Our Nation - CNA</a>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A <strong><em>roving exhibition</em></strong>, <strong><em>heritage trail</em></strong>, <strong><em>commemorative stamps</em></strong>,
and <strong><em>student competitions</em></strong> that brought Singapore's
legal story to communities and schools across the island throughout the
year.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p>3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To learn more about SGLaw200, visit <a href="http://sglaw200explore.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">sglaw200explore.sg</a>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;<strong><u>Commemorative Publication: One People, One Nation, One Rule of Law</u></strong>
</p>
<p>4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; As a fitting close to SGLaw200, "One People,
One Nation, One Rule of Law" captures the spirit of the commemoration and
invites all Singaporeans to reflect on how the Rule of Law has shaped the
nation and what it must become in the years ahead. Inspired by the national
day song "One People, One Nation, One Singapore", the publication traces
Singapore's legal journey across two centuries, from the establishment
of the Second Charter of Justice in 1826 to the legal and societal challenges
of today. Through seven chapters, it brings together historical accounts,
contemporary case studies, interviews, archival photographs and infographics
to explore the Rule of Law and its impact on Singapore's development and
everyday life. It also looks to the future with the question: how will
the Rule of Law evolve to meet the new realities Singapore faces?</p>
<p>5.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; A digital version of the publication will be
available through the SGLaw200 commemorative publication microsite at
<a href="http://www.web.gov.sg/onepeopleonenationoneruleoflaw/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">www.web.gov.sg/onepeopleonenationoneruleoflaw/</a>
</p>
