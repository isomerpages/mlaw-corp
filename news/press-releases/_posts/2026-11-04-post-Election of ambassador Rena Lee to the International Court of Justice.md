<p>3 November 2026 Posted in Press Releases</p>
<p></p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The Government of Singapore congratulates Ambassador for International
Law Rena Lee on her election as a Judge of the International Court of Justice
(ICJ) for the 2027 – 2036 term. Ambassador Lee will begin her term as Judge
of the ICJ in February 2027. Ambassador Lee is the first Singaporean and
the first woman from Southeast Asia to be elected to the ICJ. This is a
significant milestone for Singapore.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The ICJ is the United Nations’ (UN) principal judicial organ which settles
legal disputes submitted by States in accordance with international law.
It also gives advisory opinions on legal questions referred to it by authorised
UN organs and specialised agencies. It comprises 15 judges from different
UN Member States who are elected to nine-year terms.&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>On 3 November 2026, the UN held an election for five open seats on the
ICJ for the 2027 – 2036 term. Voting was conducted in both the UN General
Assembly (UNGA) and UN Security Council (UNSC) at the UN Headquarters in
New York. Ambassador Lee secured an absolute majority in both the UNGA
and UNSC, receiving 151 votes out of 191 votes in the UNGA and 13 votes
out of 15 votes in the UNSC. The strong support reflects the international
community’s strong trust and confidence in Ambassador Lee. The Government
of Singapore wishes Ambassador Lee and the incoming ICJ bench a successful
term.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Minister for Foreign Affairs Dr Vivian Balakrishnan said: “My heartiest
congratulations to Ambassador Lee on her well-deserved election to the
International Court of Justice. The results demonstrate the international
community’s recognition of Ambassador Lee’s strong abilities, extensive
experience, and standing in international law. Singapore also deeply appreciates
the votes of confidence from our many friends around the world. We are
honoured that a Singaporean is given an opportunity to serve on the ICJ
for the very first time.&nbsp; As the principal judicial organ of the United
Nations, the Court is an indispensable institution that upholds and advances
international law. The Government of Singapore has full confidence that
Ambassador Lee will discharge her duties as a Judge of the ICJ with independence
and distinction, and we look forward to her contributions to the work of
the Court.”&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Minister for Law and Second Minister for Home Affairs Mr Edwin Tong SC
said: “I am delighted to congratulate Ambassador Rena Lee on her election
to the ICJ. It was a hard-fought campaign, and the support shown by UN
Member States in New York is a strong endorsement of the international
community’s confidence in Ambassador Lee’s deep legal expertise and her
proven track record in international law. Singapore has long been a staunch
defender of an inclusive and rules-based international order. For international
law to truly work, it must be anchored in institutions that are trusted,
credible and effective. The ICJ is one such institution. For it to remain
strong and effective in upholding international law, its bench must continue
to comprise well-qualified Judges who serve with integrity. I am confident,
without reservation, that Ambassador Lee will rise to the challenge and
make a meaningful contribution to the Court.”&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ambassador Lee said: “I am deeply honoured by the trust and confidence
that the international community has placed in me. I am grateful to the
many countries that have supported my candidature. The International Court
of Justice plays a vital role in the peaceful settlement of disputes and
the upholding of international law, particularly at a time of growing global
uncertainty. I look forward to serving on the Court with independence,
impartiality and integrity, and to contributing to its important work in
the years ahead.”&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ol>
