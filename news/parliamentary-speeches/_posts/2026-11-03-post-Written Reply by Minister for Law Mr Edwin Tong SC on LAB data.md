<b><u>Name and Constituency of Member of Parliament</u></b><br>
<b>Ms Denise Phua Lay Peng (Member of Parliament for Jalan Besar GRC) </b>

<b><u>Question</u></b><br>
To ask the Minister for Law since the 2024 review of Legal Aid Bureau means test criteria (a) how many applicants who failed the means test were reviewed by a Means Test Panel; and (b) how many were granted legal aid after the review.

<b><u>Written Answer by Minister Edwin Tong SC:</u></b><br>

{:start="1"}
1.&nbsp;Under the updated means test criteria following the 2024 review, seventeen civil legal aid applicants who failed the means test were reviewed by the Means Test Panel. All seventeen had the means test requirements waived in
consideration of their respective extenuating circumstances. Of these, five applicants eventually received civil legal aid and two applicants’ cases are pending, while the remaining ten applicants eventually did not proceed with their cases.
