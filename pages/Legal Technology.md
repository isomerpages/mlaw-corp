<p><strong>Pilot of the Legal Innovation and Future-Readiness Transformation (LIFT) Initiative:</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>Through a series of in-depth surveys and consultations with the legal
industry, change management was identified as one of the top challenges
to legaltech adoption, particularly for small and medium firms.</p>
<p></p>
<p>MinLaw is piloting LIFT to support firms in diagnosing their legaltech
needs, selecting best-fit legaltech solutions, and implementing and optimising
their chosen legaltech solution.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Indicate your interest in LIFT <a href="https://go.gov.sg/liftpilot" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a> and we will contact you.</p>

<p><strong>Productivity Solutions Grant for the Legal Sector (PSG-Legal):</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>PSG-Legal provides financial support for businesses to adopt pre-scoped
IT solutions, equipment and consultancy services to improve productivity
and enhance processes with technology.</p>
<p></p>
<p>PSG-Legal was launched by MinLaw to support the legal sector in digital
transformation, which covers pre-approved solutions across various legaltech
categories.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Find out how to apply for PSG-Legal <a href="https://grants.gobusiness.gov.sg/support/productivity-solutions-grant" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Guide for Using Generative AI (GenAI) in the Legal Sector:</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>The Guide for Using GenAI in the Legal Sector was launched by MinLaw to
encourage the legal sector to leverage GenAI to enhance productivity and
remain competitive. The Guide addresses common concerns about GenAI use,
empowering law practices and in-house counsel teams to become smart buyers
and users of GenAI. These are grounded in three key principles: (a) professional
ethics, (b) confidentiality, and (c) transparency.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The Guide provides practical guidance on how law practices and in-house
legal teams can adopt GenAI while meeting professional obligations. It
includes step-by-step guidance to (a) develop an AI adoption framework,
(b) diagnose and analyse needs, (c) identify and evaluate GenAI tools,
(d) implement and train said tools, and (e) conduct review and improvement
processes.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Read more and download the Guide <a href="https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/launch-of-guide-for-using-generative-artificial-intelligence-in-the-legal-sector/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>

<p><strong>GPT-Legal, powering LawNet’s AI search engine and summarisation features</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>GPT-Legal is a collection of fine-tuned models and frameworks launched
by the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) and the Singapore Academy
of Law (SAL), and supported by MinLaw. The development of GPT-Legal involved
fine-tuning based on data samples from volunteers from the legal community,
including Justices’ Law Clerks from the Supreme Court. GPT-Legal powers
LawNet AI's summarisation and Q&amp;A features, enabling lawyers to efficiently
generate case summaries and perform legal research more efficiently and
intuitively using natural language search.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Find out more about GPT-Legal <a href="https://www.lawnet.com" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Singapore Open Legal Informatics Database (SOLID):</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>SOLID, developed by the Singapore Management University (SMU), with support
by MinLaw, is Singapore’s first comprehensive open-source database of structured
legal data on Singapore’s cases, statutes, courts and legal scholarship.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The three-year project was officially launched on 18 November 2025 and
enables rigorous computational and empirical studies of Singapore law by
both local and international scholars. It also supports the development
and evaluation of legaltech and AI systems.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Read more about SOLID <a href="https://news.smu.edu.sg/news/2025/11/18/smu-build-open-legal-database-support-ministry-law" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Cybersecurity Health Clinic for Law Firms:</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p>The Cybersecurity Health Clinic for Law Firms is jointly organised by
MinLaw and the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore under the LegalTech GoWhere
(LGW) initiative. This programme is specially designed for small and medium
Singapore law practices, providing legal professionals with practical guidance
tailored to the legal industry's specific requirements, helping firms to
strengthen their cybersecurity postures and protect sensitive client information.</p>
