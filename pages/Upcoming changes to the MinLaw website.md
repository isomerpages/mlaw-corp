<h3><strong>Experience a better and more user-friendly MinLaw website</strong></h3>
<p>As part of the Ministry’s effort to enhance our digital touchpoints and
your user experience, our website (<a href="http://www.mlaw.gov.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">www.mlaw.gov.sg</a>) is getting a new look
on 30 November 2026.</p>
<p>We have worked closely with key stakeholders to review the current design
and contents, including moving information from some of our microsites
to this new site, so that you can find what you need more easily, in one
place.</p>
<p>With the launch of our refreshed website on 30 November 2026, there will
be changes to the URLs of some of current microsites listed below. If you
have previously saved the URLs as bookmarks, do update them.</p>
