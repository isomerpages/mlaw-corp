<p><strong>ABOUT THE PROGRAMME</strong>
</p>
<p>The Singapore-Shanghai Lawyers Exchange Programme was introduced in 2023,
following the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding by the Singapore
Ministry of Law, the Shanghai Municipal Bureau of Justice, The Law Society
of Singapore and the Shanghai Bar Association.</p>
<p>The programme aims to facilitate knowledge exchange, and promote understanding
of the business environment and culture between the two cities.</p>
<p>It comprises two separate programmes:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>A two-month attachment, where Singapore-qualified lawyers are attached
to Chinese law firms in Shanghai for two months. Funding support is available.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A one-week study visit, where senior Singapore lawyers visit selected
Shanghai law firms, institutions, businesses and agencies, and engage with
potential clients and partners.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>WHY IT MATTERS</strong>
</p>
<p>China is the second largest economy in the world, and one of Singapore’s
most important trade and investment partners. As Singapore enterprises
expand their presence in China, and Chinese companies increasingly use
Singapore as a gateway into Southeast Asia and global markets, the scale
and sophistication of cross-border commercial activity is expected to grow.&nbsp;</p>
<p>In this environment, lawyers in both countries must navigate not only
different legal systems, but also distinct regulatory frameworks, business
cultures, and professional practices. The Singapore–Shanghai Lawyers Exchange
Programme offers a structured and valuable platform for practitioners to
gain first-hand exposure to how law is practised in each jurisdiction,
deepen their understanding of local legal frameworks, and build trusted
professional networks.</p>
<p>Through these exchanges, lawyers develop practical, on-the-ground insights
that strengthen their ability to advise clients on complex cross-border
matters, manage legal and commercial risk more effectively, and support
the continued growth of trade and investment between Singapore, China,
and beyond.</p>

<p><strong>WHO CAN PARTICIPATE</strong>
</p>
<p>Any Singapore citizen or permanent resident who:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Is an advocate and solicitor of the Supreme Court of Singapore</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Is working in a Singapore law practice and nominated by the firm</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Has at least two years of post-qualification experience is preferred</p>
</li>
</ul>
